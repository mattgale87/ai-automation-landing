How This Fits an AI Security Stack

  • YubiKey → admin access. Closes the #1 way AI infra gets owned: phishing → credential theft → pivot to model repo. Hardware-bound means a stolen password alone is useless.
  • Webcam cover → PII capture defense. Less about "spy paranoia" and more about compliance: EU AI Act and most privacy laws treat remote-camera capture as a special category. A cover is the cleanest technical control.
  • USB data blocker → juice-jacking defense. Neutralizes the physical-channel attacks your software stack can't see. Mandatory if your team travels with hardware that touches customer data.
