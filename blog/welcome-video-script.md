"Your AI agent can delete your entire CRM with one prompt. I convinced one to do it in 4 seconds. And nobody noticed for 8 months."

## FULL SCRIPT (~60-75 seconds)

**[SCENE 1 — HOOK] (0:00-0:06)**
"Your AI agent can delete your entire CRM with one prompt. I convinced one to do it in 4 seconds. And nobody noticed for 8 months."

**[SCENE 2 — CREDIBILITY] (0:06-0:18)**
"I'm Matt Gale. 15 years in enterprise IT. I've managed 80M dollar budgets, 40-person teams, and I currently run GaleOps where we audit AI systems for businesses deploying agents into production."

**[SCENE 3 — THE PROBLEM] (0:18-0:32)**
"Here's what keeps me up at night: traditional cybersecurity was never designed for AI. Your firewall doesn't check prompt injections. Your SOC doesn't flag when an agent exports your entire customer database. And your compliance team? They're still running last century's playbook."

**[SCENE 4 — WHAT WE COVER] (0:32-0:45)**
"On this channel, we cover three things: AI security — what gets tested and why your stack is vulnerable. Enterprise IT strategy — honest takes on the tools shaping modern tech. And AI automation — how to deploy agents without getting burned."

**[SCENE 5 — PROOF] (0:45-0:55)**
"We've found vulnerabilities in production systems that had been running for months. We've tested agents that could be tricked into deleting 200 records in seconds. And we've mapped the exact frameworks you need to be compliant."

**[SCENE 6 — CTA] (0:55-1:05)**
"If you deploy AI agents, manage IT infrastructure, or just want to stay ahead of the security curve — subscribe. New videos every week. And if you want a free 15-minute consultation on your AI stack, the link is in the description."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Tone:** Direct, authoritative, slightly urgent but not alarmist
**Pacing:** Fast cuts, no dead air
**Visual Style:** Dark background (#08090a), cyan (#00D4FF) accents, white text
**Music:** Subtle tech ambient, builds slightly at the hook
**Captions:** Bold, positioned in safe zone (bottom 17%)

**Key Retention Techniques Used:**
1. Pattern interrupt hook (shocking stat)
2. Credibility establishment within 18 seconds
3. Problem agitation (not fear-mongering, but real stakes)
4. Clear value proposition (3 content pillars)
5. Social proof (real findings)
6. Low-friction CTA (subscribe + free consultation link)
