Enterprise Trust

AI security from someone who's run production at scale

Most AI security consultants have never managed a 5,000-location network or governed an $80M technology portfolio. I have.

AI security is not a new discipline. It's the same fundamentals — identity, access control, data boundaries, monitoring, incident response — applied to a new attack surface. The difference is speed: AI agents can be exploited in milliseconds through prompt injection, and the attack surface changes every time you update a system prompt.

I spent 14 years building and securing enterprise infrastructure at Wells Fargo, The Tile Shop, and MidHudson Regional Hospital. I've governed $80M+ technology portfolios, led 40-person engineering organizations, and managed relationships with 20+ of the world's largest technology vendors. I've integrated Splunk, AppDynamics, and SevOne into operations workflows. I've driven 40% MTTR reductions. I've negotiated $2M+ in carrier savings.

When I test your AI agent, I'm not running a checklist. I'm applying the same adversarial mindset I used to secure financial services networks, retail point-of-sale systems, and hospital clinical networks — because the attack surface is different, but the discipline is the same.
