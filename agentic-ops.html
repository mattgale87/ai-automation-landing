FAQ

Common questions

What is Agentic Ops?
Agentic Ops is the operational layer that keeps AI agents running in production: cost governance, reliability and observability, and incident response. It's the "how you run the fleet" that platform vendors don't provide.
How is this different from buying Langfuse or LangSmith?
Those are observability tools. We operate them for you and deliver the outcome — lower cost, higher reliability, faster incident response. The software is an input, not the deliverable.
Do you replace our existing agent platform?
No. We work with whatever you've built — LangChain, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, or a custom stack. We operate on top of your existing tools.
What does the free Agent Cost Audit give me?
A 15-minute call where we look at your agent fleet and LLM bill, then a one-page report on where your tokens actually go, what's wasting spend, and where ceilings should sit.
How fast do I see cost savings?
Most clients see a measurable reduction in the first billing cycle after we put ceilings, caching, and routing in place. The re-sent-context and loop fixes show up within a week.
Do you need access to our systems?
For the audit, a read-only view of your token/cost usage is enough. For a retainer, we need the access level required to monitor and respond to your agents — scoped to least privilege.
What if my agents are already broken?
That's exactly what incident response is for. We diagnose what went wrong, contain it, and put controls in place so it doesn't recur.
Can you do this for a company my size?
Yes. The audit and Starter retainer are built for teams running a handful of agents. Scale is for larger fleets. We scale the engagement to what you actually run.
Do you build agents or run them?
We run them. If you need agents built, we can point you to the right platform or builder, but our job is the operations layer — keeping what you have reliable and affordable.
What happens after the audit?
You get the report either way. If you want us to implement the fixes and keep running the fleet, we move to a retainer. No pressure, no lock-in.
