## Homepage hero subhead (copywriting — specificty over vague)
**Before:** "Start with a fast human review of your AI system. I break agents before attackers do, show you exactly what failed, and hand you the fixes in a 15-minute walkthrough and PDF."
**After:** "Most AI agents go live with zero security review. I break them before attackers do — prompt injection, data leakage, rogue tool calls — then hand you the exact fixes in a 15-minute walkthrough and PDF."

Principle applied: led with a specific, relatable pain (zero security review) + named threat vectors (concrete > vague "what failed").

## Service-page tier comparison (pricing — decoy/middle-tier psychology)
Added to /ai-security-audit/ a "Which service do I need?" block:
- **$5K · Audit** — "Start here." (entry, anchors the range)
- **$8K · Guardrails** — marked **MOST CHOSEN** (gold border, the decoy/middle tier that should win)
- **$12K · Red Team** — "You need proof." (premium, justifies the anchor)

Pricing principles applied:
- **Anchoring:** showing all 3 together makes $5K feel accessible after seeing $12K.
- **Decoy effect:** $8K is the recommended middle — most chosen.
- **Round pricing:** $5K/$8K/$12K (premium signal, not charm-pricing).
- **Value metric:** flat fee (Basecamp model) — right for a consultancy, no per-seat games.
