Common questions.

If yours isn't here, email mattgale87@gmail.com.

How fast do I get the report?

5 business days from when Stripe confirms payment. You'll get a kickoff email within 24 hours confirming the timeline and any clarifying questions. If a rush is needed (CTO demo Friday, audit deadline, etc.), email after you submit and we'll see if we can compress.

What if I don't have a sample interaction?

No problem — it's optional. We'll run a broader set of generic probes against your system prompt alone. If you have an example, even a single representative back-and-forth, the review is materially sharper. Just paste it in the optional field.

Do you keep my system prompt confidential?

Yes. Your prompt and any sample interactions are used only for your review, stored on encrypted storage, and deleted within 30 days of delivery. NDA available on request before submission — just email after you pay. We never share, never post, never use as a marketing example.

What happens if I disagree with a finding?

Email with the finding ID and your reasoning. We re-test against your clarification. If we still believe it's a real risk, we'll explain why with a concrete reproduction. If it turns out to be a false positive, you don't pay for that finding — no questions asked.

Can I share the PDF with my team?

Yes. White-label it forward to your CTO, your compliance officer, your board. It's branded for your team on purpose. We don't put a GaleOps watermark on the deliverables — they look like an internal document you commissioned.

Why manual review and not automated?

Automated scanners catch maybe 30% of what matters. The other 70% is multi-turn setup attacks, encoding chains, tool-call escalation, and persona-shift — stuff that only a human reading your prompt with context catches. The $149 is the labor of one researcher spending 4-6 hours on your specific agent.
