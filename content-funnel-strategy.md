# GaleTech Cross-Platform Content Funnel Strategy

## Platform Roles

| Platform | Role | Content Type | Funnel Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YouTube** | Hub | Long-form (5-15 min) | Deep dives, authority building, ad revenue |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Discovery | Vertical 60s clips | New audience acquisition, algorithm push |
| **X/Twitter** | Distribution | Threads + single posts | Drive traffic to YouTube, engage tech community |
| **Bluesky** | Community | Short posts + engagement | Build tech credibility, network with IT pros |
| **Instagram** | Visual | Reels + carousels | Repurpose video clips, reach broader audience |
| **TikTok** | Growth | Short vertical clips | Maximum reach, drive to YouTube/website |
| **galeops.xyz** | Conversion | Landing pages + blog | Capture leads, sell services |

## Funnel Flow

```
TikTok/Shorts (Discovery)
    → X/Bluesky (Engagement)
        → YouTube (Deep Content)
            → galeops.xyz (Conversion/Consultation)
                → Email List (Retention)
                    → All platforms (Re-engagement)
```
