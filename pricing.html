FAQ

Common pricing questions

What if I don't know which service I need?
Book a free 15-minute fit call. We'll map your AI surface, your deployment context, and which engagement tier matches your exposure. No commitment required.
Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?
Yes. The $750 Founding AI Agent Risk Review is the entry point for early-stage teams. The full fee is credited toward a $5,000 audit when you're ready to scale.
What payment methods do you accept?
Credit card via Stripe for all published services. Invoicing and net-30 terms are available for enterprise engagements over $10,000.
Can I start with a retainer without a baseline assessment?
The retainer builds on a baseline. Start with the $750 Risk Review or the $3,500 MCP Assessment — findings become the first living risk register and the regression set for future checks.
What if the assessment finds no critical issues?
You still receive a written account of what was tested, the controls that held, and the risks to monitor as your agent changes. A clean result is useful evidence, not a failed engagement.
