About

Enterprise IT leader turned AI security specialist

14+ years building, securing, and scaling enterprise infrastructure at Wells Fargo, Tile Shop, and MidHudson Regional Hospital. Now I break AI agents before attackers do.

I spent 14 years running enterprise IT for some of the most regulated environments in the country — financial services, healthcare, retail, and higher education. The same security disciplines I used there apply directly to AI agents today.

At Wells Fargo, I led a 40-person engineering organization, governed an $80M+ technology portfolio, and directed a multi-year SD-WAN transformation across 5,000+ locations. I integrated Splunk, AppDynamics, and SevOne into operations — driving a 40% reduction in mean time to resolution. I negotiated $2M+ in carrier savings and established roadmaps for Zero Trust, hybrid cloud, and network modernization.

At The Tile Shop, I held full responsibility for enterprise infrastructure across 370+ retail locations. I led a Juniper-to-Meraki SD-WAN migration that cut WAN costs by 20%, redesigned store connectivity with LTE failover, and delivered SOX-compliant security controls.

At MidHudson Regional Hospital, I ran network engineering for 24x7 clinical operations — EMR, PACS, life-safety systems — under HIPAA. I cut patient-impacting network events from over two hours to under 30 minutes and achieved 99.99% availability for critical hospital systems.

Now I apply that same discipline to AI security. Prompt injection, tool misuse, data leakage — these are the same attack surface problems I've been solving for 14 years, just with a new protocol layer on top.
