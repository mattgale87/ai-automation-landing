## Market Rate Context (from securitywall.co / ghost.securitywall.co)
- **Honest 2026 market range: $6,000 – $45,000+** for LLM pentest / AI red team.
- Bottom (~$6K): single chatbot, no tools, no compliance attachment.
- Top (~$45K): complex multi-agent systems, RAG, function calling, persistent memory, compliance audit.
- **Implication**: GaleOps at $5K is *below* the stated market floor. Either we're underpricing, or we should reposition $5K as "entry/single-surface" and push $8K/$12K as the real engagements.

### 3. DeepAssure — deepassure.ai

### 4. Zeaynx — zeaynx.io

- **Offering**: Red team (chains), MCP security audit, indirect injection, tool misuse, long-running agent loops.
- **Threat**: Cheap entry ($500) + subscription model = agile. MCP/agentic focus is ahead of most.

## Strategic Takeaways for GaleOps

### Where we're weak vs them
1. **Price**: Our $5K audit is HIGHER than AI Security Web ($3K) and Zeaynx ($500 entry). We lose on raw price.
2. **Interactive scanner polish**: AI Security Web's 3-tool scanner looks more polished than our /scan/ landing.
3. **Framework badges**: DeepAssure name-drops NIST/OWASP/ISO/EU AI Act. We don't visibly claim OWASP LLM Top 10 alignment — we should.

### Where we're strong
1. **Bug-bounty credibility**: Mathew's GhostInTheCode (Bugcrowd) + OpenAI Safety Bounty target history = real offensive cred they can't claim.
2. **The $149 snapshot**: unique low-friction paid entry they don't have (they have free scans, not a cheap paid deliverable).
3. **Flat-fee simplicity**: $5K/$8K/$12K is easier to buy than "request a quote" (Red AI Team, DeepAssure).
4. **Video/content**: We now have 5 narrated videos; none of them have video funnels.

### Opportunities (gaps to exploit)
1. **OWASP LLM Top 10 alignment page** — they hint, we claim explicitly + map our audit to it.
2. **"AI Security Web alternative" / "Red AI Team alternative" comparison pages** (SEO-competitor-pages skill) — capture their branded search intent.
3. **Raise the $5K audit to $6K** (market floor) OR reposition it as "single-surface entry" and lead with $8K guardrails as the real engagement. Market data says $6K is the bottom — we're leaving money on the table at $5K.
4. **Agentic/MCP security angle** — Zeaynx owns this narrative. GaleOps should publish an MCP/agentic red-team offer (we have the skill: agentic systems are exactly what the $12K red team covers).

### Recommended pricing move
- Keep $149 snapshot (unique, keep as funnel).
- **$5K → $6K** (matches market floor, +20% margin, still cheaper than AI Security Web's $18K "traditional" anchor and competitive with their $3K on value).
- $8K guardrails = "most chosen" (already marked).
- $12K red team = "proof/compliance" (already anchored).
