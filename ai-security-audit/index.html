Mapped to OWASP LLM Top 10

Every finding is traced to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025) so your team and regulators speak the same language:

  • LLM01 — Prompt Injection — direct & indirect instruction overrides via retrieved content
  • LLM02 — Sensitive Information Disclosure — PII, secrets, and training-data leakage through responses
  • LLM03 — Supply Chain — vulnerable plugins, packages, and third-party model dependencies
  • LLM05 — Improper Output Handling — XSS / injection via unfiltered model output
  • LLM06 — Excessive Agency — dangerous tool/function permissions granted to autonomous agents
  • LLM08 — Excessive Permission — over-broad access granted to the model or its tools

Full coverage matrix (LLM01–LLM10) is in the written report. NIST AI RMF and EU AI Act articles are cross-referenced where relevant.
